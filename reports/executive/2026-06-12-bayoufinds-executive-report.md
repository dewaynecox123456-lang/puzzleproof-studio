The current funnel shows top-of-funnel visibility without completed purchase activity.

- Facebook views are meaningful at 20,095.
- Unique viewer count is meaningful at 5,923.
- Payhip product views show product-market curiosity.
- Orders remain at 0.

This suggests that the audience is discovering BayouFinds offers, but the offer pages, pricing, proof, call-to-action, or trust layer are not yet converting attention into transactions.

## 5. Conversion Risk

The primary business risk is not lack of audience. The risk is conversion friction.

Current conversion risk signals:

- $0 in monthly sales despite product page traffic.
- 0 orders despite multiple software products receiving views.
- 0 one-minute video views, suggesting limited deep engagement from current video content.
- Product interest is spread across several offers, which may dilute the strongest buying path.

If conversion is not addressed, BayouFinds may continue to generate attention without revenue capture.

## 6. Strategic Recommendation

Prioritize software products and tighten conversion paths.

Recommended focus:

- Keep PuzzleProof Studio and BayouOps products as the primary product direction.
- Treat legacy product categories as secondary unless they directly support software credibility or audience trust.
- Build clearer product pages with stronger problem statements, proof, screenshots, and buyer outcomes.
- Create a simple early-access path that reduces uncertainty for testers and buyers.
- Use Facebook reach to drive one focused offer at a time instead of spreading attention across too many products.

## 8. Final Executive Note

BayouFinds has audience movement and product curiosity. The next growth step is not more product sprawl; it is sharper conversion around the strongest software offers.

Software products are showing the clearest path forward. PuzzleProof Studio and BayouOps should remain the strategic priority while BayouFinds improves trust, proof, and purchase flow.
