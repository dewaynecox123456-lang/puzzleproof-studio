# Feature Governance Policy

PuzzleProof Studio must remain focused on puzzle production workflows.

Every feature must support at least one of these outcomes:

- Protect the artist
- Protect Sean and Wonder Piece Studio
- Preserve copyright information
- Improve production
- Improve cataloging
- Improve printing
- Improve reprints
- Improve manufacturing

Do not add features for:

- Social media management
- Accounting
- CRM workflows
- AI generation
- General image editing beyond production needs
- Non-puzzle production workflows

When in doubt, leave the feature out until it can be tied directly to the approved workflow.
